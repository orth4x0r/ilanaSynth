# Rebuild of the 8 presets marked bad in both forms (2026-09-30)

The owner's blind A/B marked these eight bad in their old and their new (Airwindows-voiced) form, so each was rebuilt from scratch, not re-voiced. Their `PresetVoicing.h` lines are deleted, nothing is layered on, names and parameter ids are unchanged, and each has four new macros. Five of them live in the original 80 (host program numbers): their old recipes in `Presets.h` are stubs and `Library::rebuildLegacy` in `PresetLibrary.h` swaps the new ones in by name. Levels: every preset within 1 dB rms of its row in `tests/fingerprints-linux.csv`, peak at most 1 dB above. Filter notes from the renders: AW Acid (32) leaks broadband hash at any cutoff (spectral centroid stays near 3 kHz), and AW X LP (33) is 15 dB quieter than the others, so neither is used.

**Rip Bass (Bass).** A Growl wavetable saw (3 voices, narrow) with a sine FM modulator one octave up held out of the mix: a fast mod envelope drives both the FM amount and the table frame, so every note starts with an FM "rip" that settles into a clean growl. Sine sub under it, AW YNot LP (300 Hz, resonance 0.7, 2.6 octaves of filter envelope, velocity-sensitive), mono with a short glide. A tempo-synced triangle LFO on the cutoff is gated by macro 3. FX: Drive, Airwindows Inflamer, EQ (low cut, presence), Limiter. Macros: TONE (cutoff and morph), RIP (FM and frame), WUB (LFO depth), GRIT (drive and filter drive).

**Glitch Lead (Lead).** A Bit Ramp table with a quantising warp and a Wavecrush octave layer. Two sample-and-hold LFOs (1/16 and 1/8) step the table frame and the cutoff in time, so the timbre jumps without touching pitch. AW Angle filter (2.2 kHz, resonance 0.6). FX: Crush, a 16-step trance gate, ping-pong delay, small plate. Macros: GLITCH (warp amount and the second oscillator), CRUSH (crush mix and bit depth), CHOP (gate depth), TONE (cutoff and morph).

**Rig Lead (Lead).** A guitar-rig solo voice: Soft Saw (two detuned voices) over a soft square an octave down, AW Holt filter opening on the pick (1.5 octaves), legato glide with a 2-semitone bend. The vibrato fades in on the mod envelope (0.5 s) and the macro adds more. FX: tube amp, delay, spring reverb, limiter. Macros: DRIVE (amp drive and filter drive), TONE (cutoff and amp treble), VIBRATO (LFO to pitch), SPACE (delay and reverb).

**Octave Screamer (Lead).** A Saw Octaves table in five Octaves-mode unison voices, plus a Drive Saw an octave up in Fifths-mode unison. A tube-screamer chain: AW Z LP, then EQ with a mid hump around 900 Hz and the low end cut, Drive, Airwindows Inflamer, delay, plate, limiter. Wheel vibrato and a slow table-frame sweep. Macros: OCTAVES (blend and octave-up level), SCREAM (drive and filter drive), TONE (cutoff and morph), SPACE.

**Sync Scream (Lead).** True hard sync: a master saw held out of the mix resets a slave saw (two voices) whose pitch is swept by a mod envelope from about 14 semitones above, decaying to 3 over 1.6 s, with a slow LFO and the wheel on top (the classic sync scream). AW Z LP (3.5 kHz, velocity-sensitive), a square sub, tube amp, dotted delay, plate, limiter. Macros: SYNC (sweep range and cutoff), SCREAM (amp drive and resonance), TONE, SPACE.

**Six-Op Organ (Keys).** Six sine operators at Hammond drawbar ratios (16', 8', 5 1/3', 4', 2', plus 2 2/3' as the percussion operator on the mod envelope, which gives the decaying key click), a little FM feedback and a cross cell for a reedy edge. AW Z LP (4.5 kHz) and an AW Z HP at 40 Hz, which removes the DC the FM feedback leaves. FX: tube amp, chorus and tremolo as the Leslie, spring reverb, limiter. Macros: DRAWBARS (upper operators), PERC (percussion level and decay), LESLIE (tremolo depth and chorus), DIRT (amp drive and FM feedback). The organ has no velocity response, as a real one has none (the critic flags it).

**Quad Mod Pluck (Pluck).** A Pluck Stiff table with an FM Stack an octave up. Four modulators move it at once: the mod envelope sweeps the table frame down (and the second frame up), a per-note random source offsets the frame, velocity opens the filter, and a per-voice smooth-random LFO breathes the layer's level. AW Holt filter (500 Hz, 3.5 octaves of envelope, resonance 0.4). FX: Airwindows ChorusEnsemble, ping-pong delay, plate, limiter (it keeps the pluck's crest factor inside the level rule). Macros: BITE (cutoff, resonance, drive), MOTION (frame and FM layer), DECAY, SPACE.

**Resonator Pluck (Pluck).** Two physical strings (a noise-burst one in two voices and a burst one an octave up) are coupled into a tuned Bar resonator (string to body, resonator amount 0.85), so the body rings on after the string dies. An AW Holt filter opens on the strike. Delay and room. Macros: BODY (resonator amount and coupling), DECAY (resonator and string), BRIGHT (cutoff and string damping), SPACE. In the batch renderer this preset (like the existing string-and-body presets, e.g. Bell Shell Hybrid) shows a level that depends on what rendered before it (note-to-note differences of tens of dB after certain renders), so the critic's macro and velocity numbers for it are unreliable; measured on fresh processes each macro changes the sound clearly.
