Airwindows algorithms in ilanaSynth
===================================

The files in this folder other than Algorithm.h, Registry.h and Registry.cpp
are ports of Airwindows plugins by Chris Johnson (https://www.airwindows.com,
https://github.com/airwindows/airwindows). Their per-sample processing code and
state are Chris's; only the VST plugin glue was removed, by
tools/port_airwindows.py. Thank you, Chris.

Ported: ToTape6, IronOxide5, Tape, Density, Drive, Spiral2, PurestDrive,
Tube2, Mojo, Coils2, Console7Channel, Console7Buss, Channel9, Air, Air3,
Capacitor2, Baxandall2, Isolator2, Holt2, Pressure5, ButterComp2, Logical4,
Pyewacket, Pop2, Galactic, kCathedral, Verbity2, Chamber, MatrixVerb, Wider,
Srsly2, ToVinyl4, DeRez2, Deckwrecka, BitShiftGain, DrumSlam, Inflamer,
Flutter, ChorusEnsemble.
